Delete your DuxOS Society account or data

App: DuxOS Society · Publisher: DuxOS · Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can ask DuxOS to delete your DuxOS Society account and the personal data linked to it, or to delete only some of your data and keep your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@duxos.in with the subject "Delete my DuxOS Society account". Use the email address or write from the phone number that is on your account, so we can confirm it is you.
  2. In the message, give your full name, your society name and your flat or unit.
  3. Say whether you want your whole account deleted, or only some data deleted (and which).
  4. We will confirm your identity, and we will reply once the request has been completed, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your profile details: name, phone number and email address;
  • your login, sign-in sessions and registered devices, including the notification token;
  • photos and files you uploaded, such as payment screenshots.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Accounting and billing records of your housing society, such as maintenance bills and recorded payments, which your society must keep for its books and for legal reasons. Where possible these are kept without your personal details.
  • Security and audit records of actions in the app, such as visitor entries, kept for the period your society and the law require.

Records kept for these reasons are not used for any other purpose, and are deleted when the retention period ends.

Questions

Write to admin@duxos.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
