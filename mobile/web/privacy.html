Privacy Policy

DuxOS Society (Android package in.duxos.society) · Last updated: 12 November 2026

DuxOS Society is a housing society management app published by DuxOS ("we", "us"). It is used by residents, security staff, technical staff and committee members of a housing society. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who controls your data

Your housing society sets up the accounts and decides who uses the app. DuxOS provides the software and hosts the data on its servers. For questions about your data, write to admin@duxos.in.

2. Information the app handles

  • Account and profile details: name, phone number, email address, role (for example resident, security, committee) and the flat or unit you are linked to. These are entered by your society's administrators or by you.
  • Vehicle details registered to your flat, where your society records them.
  • Visitor records: details of visitors that the security desk enters, such as visitor name, phone number and purpose of visit, and your approve or reject decision.
  • Maintenance bills and payment records: the app only records payments made outside the app. It does not take payments and does not collect card, bank or UPI credentials.
  • Photos and files you choose to upload, for example a screenshot of a payment, or a photo you take with the camera when attaching one. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token that identifies your device so we can send you alerts, such as a visitor waiting at the gate.
  • Login and activity records: sign-in sessions and an audit log of actions taken in the app, kept for security and for your society's records.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising.

3. Biometric unlock

If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the check is done by your phone's operating system. Your biometric data is not sent to us and is not stored by the app.

4. How we use the information

  • to run the society's day-to-day services in the app (visitors, billing records, notices, requests and similar);
  • to send you notifications about those services;
  • to keep accounts secure and investigate misuse;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

5. Who can see it

Information is visible to the people in your society who need it for their role, for example the committee and administrators, and the security desk for visitor information. We use service providers to run the app: cloud hosting for our servers and database, and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. They handle data only to provide those services. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access is controlled by login and role. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information while your society uses the service and as needed for its records and for legal reasons. You can ask us to correct your details, or to delete your account and the personal data linked to it, by writing to admin@duxos.in from the email address or phone number on your account. Some records, such as accounting entries that your society must keep, may be retained in a form that is required by law or by your society.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
