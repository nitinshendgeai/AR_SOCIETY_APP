How to use DUX OS

A step-by-step guide for everyone in the society: residents, security guards, staff, managers, the committee and the admin. Pick your role above to see only what you need, or search for the task you have in mind.

App address: society.duxos.in  ·  Works in any browser on a phone or a computer

Contents

    Contents

      Getting started

      everyone

      Sign in, find your way around and keep your account safe.

      Sign in

      1. Open society.duxos.in in your browser.
      2. Type your email or mobile number and your password, then press Sign in.
      3. The first time, the app asks you to choose a new password and accept the terms. The society admin also sees a short Setup Wizard (see Setting up the society).
      TipIf you forgot your password, ask the society admin. They open Administration → Users & Roles, find you and reset it. Every device you were signed in on is signed out, and you sign in with the new password.

      Finding your way around

      • On a computer, the menu is on the left, grouped under People, Community, Operations, Finance, Administration and My Account. Press Collapse to make it narrow.
      • On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top left. A page opened from a link shows a slim ← Dashboard bar so you can always get back.
      • You only see what your role needs. A resident sees My Bills and Complaints, a guard sees the gate screens, the admin sees everything. If a screen you expect is missing, ask the admin (the admin can grant it under Administration → Forms Matrix).
      • Your name at the top right opens the account menu: change password, Signed-in devices and sign out.

      Your dashboard

      The first page after sign-in shows the numbers that matter for your role, and chips for the things you do most. For example, a resident sees Open Complaints, Visitors Waiting, Bills Due and My Flat. A guard sees Log Visitor, Check In, Check Out and Vehicle Gate.

      For residents and tenants

      resident · tenant

      Visitors, complaints, bills, your details and your vehicle.

      Approve or reject a visitor

      When the guard logs someone who is coming to your flat, you are asked to approve them.

      1. On your dashboard, open Approvals (a number on the chip shows how many are waiting), or Community → Visitors.
      2. Check the name and purpose, then press Approve or Reject.
      3. The guard sees your answer straight away and lets the visitor in, or not.

      Raise a complaint

      1. Open Community → Complaints and press New complaint.
      2. Pick a category, describe the problem and save.
      3. Follow it on the same screen. A complaint goes Open → Assigned → In progress → Resolved → Closed. If the work is not done properly, press Reopen.

      See your bills

      Open My Account → My Bills. Each bill lists every charge (maintenance, sinking fund, parking and so on), the due date and what is still unpaid. Open a bill to see the full break-up. The dashboard card Bills Due shows the amount outstanding.

      Pay a bill

      Open Finance → Payments and press Record Payment. Pick your flat, choose Open bills, oldest first (or One bill), enter the amount, the date and how you paid with the cheque or reference number, and attach a photo of the cheque or receipt if you have one. Press Save & Generate Receipt. Payments settle your oldest bill first, and anything extra is kept as an advance against the next bill.

      Update your details

      Open My Account → Edit My Info, change what you need and save. Your change shows “Change request pending approval” until the admin or a committee member approves it, so the society's records stay correct.

      Register your vehicle

      Add your vehicle (number plate, type) from the Vehicles section of your profile. The number is saved in capital letters without spaces, so mh 12 ab-1234 becomes MH12AB1234. It appears in Parking Management straight away. If your society gives you a slot, the slot number shows on the vehicle.

      NoteYou can register a number only once in the society. A second car with no slot is still shown to the guard as registered, with no parking.

      For security guards

      security

      The gate: visitors and vehicles.

      Log a visitor

      1. On your dashboard press Log Visitor.
      2. Enter the visitor's name, phone, purpose, the flat they are visiting and the vehicle number if they have one.
      3. Save. The resident of that flat is asked to approve.
      4. When the resident approves, open Check In and check the visitor in. When they leave, open Check Out. The full list is under Visitor Log.
      Logged→Waiting for resident→Approved→Checked in→Checked out

      If the resident rejects, entry is denied. A visitor who is still waiting for approval, was rejected, or has already left is shown in red on the vehicle check.

      Check a vehicle at the gate

      1. Press Vehicle Gate on your dashboard.
      2. Type the number plate (capitals or small, with or without spaces and dashes) or scan it with a handheld or USB scanner, and press Enter.
      3. Read the colour of the answer:
      AnswerMeansYou do
      AllowedA resident or tenant vehicle that has parking today, or an approved visitor.Let it in. Press Log Entry.
      Registered, no parkingThe vehicle belongs to the society but has no slot (a second car, an expired rental).Send it elsewhere, or call the owner.
      Not registeredThe number is not known.Verify by hand before allowing entry.

      Press Log Entry or Log Exit to record the movement. Just looking a number up records nothing. After each check, press Check Another Vehicle.

      Your attendance

      Open Operations → Staff (Staff Portal) and press Attendance to punch in at the start of the shift and out at the end. A night shift works too: punch in at 22:00 and out at 06:00 next day. Your supervisor approves each punch. If you are still punched in, you must check out before you can punch in again.

      Use your own loginDo not share one “guard” login among the team. The attendance and gate records name the login, so a shared one cannot show who did what.

      For staff

      housekeeping · technical · gym

      Your day in the Staff Portal: Operations → Staff.

      Attendance

      Punch in when you start and out when you finish. Time is the society's clock. More than 30 minutes after the shift start counts as late.

      My Duties

      The duties given to you today. Open one and tick each checklist item as you finish it.

      Handover

      Leave notes for the next shift: what is done, what is pending, anything to watch.

      Tasks

      Complaints and jobs assigned to you. Mark them in progress and then resolved.

      If you forgot to punch

      Ask your supervisor. You can request a status correction on a past day (for example, absent to present). The supervisor approves or rejects it. To fix the actual times, the supervisor enters the day from the printed sheet.

      Paper sheets

      Your supervisor may give you a printed duty sheet. Tick each item as you finish it and write your in and out times. The supervisor types the sheet into the app. See For supervisors and managers.

      For supervisors and managers

      supervisor · manager

      Approve the team's day, give duties, print the sheets.

      Approve punches

      Open Approvals on your dashboard. Every punch-in and punch-out of your team waits here. Approve or reject each. A rejected punch does not count as present, and the person can punch in again. Your dashboard also shows Pending check-in, Pending punch-out, Absent and Late counts.

      Set up checklist templates (once)

      Open Operations → Checklist Templates and create a template for each routine, such as “Floor cleaning” with its items (sweep, mop, dustbins…). Duties copy the items from the template.

      Give duties

      1. Press Assign Duty.
      2. Under Assign to, pick one or several staff. Chips are grouped by department, with Select all for each.
      3. Optionally pick a Checklist template. Fill the duty name, location and the Duty date.
      4. For a daily round, switch on Repeat on more days, choose Until (up to 62 days) and tick the weekdays.
      5. Set start and end times if you want, then save.

      Print the duty sheets

      From the duties screens you can print a sheet for one staff member, for a whole department, or a blank sheet for a template. A supervisor sees and prints only their own department.

      Housekeeping, floor by floorThe housekeeping sheet is one page per staff member for each wing: the floors are the rows (a 23-floor building fits one page) and the template items are tick columns. Three staff means three pages a day.

      Enter a filled-in sheet

      Open Paper sheet entry, pick the day and the staff, then enter the ticks and the in and out times from the paper. Entries made by a supervisor are approved already.

      Corrections and handover

      Attendance Corrections lists the status changes your team asked for. Approve or reject them. Handover shows the notes left between shifts.

      Complaints

      The manager opens Complaints, assigns each one to a department or person, and follows it to Resolved and Closed.

      Setting up the society

      admin · committee

      Do this once, in this order. Administration → Setup Wizard walks you through it.

      1 Society profile→2 Wings→3 Floors→4 Flats→5 Users & roles

      Society profile

      Administration → Society Settings: name, address, bank details for bills, the society's time zone (India by default). Staff punches use this clock.

      Wings, floors and flats

      Add each wing, then its floors, then the flats with their area in sq ft (area-based charges need it). The Setup Wizard has a button for each step.

      Residents and tenants

      People → Residents and People → Tenants: add one by one, or import a list. Open a flat to see who lives there, their vehicles and their dues. When someone leaves, open their profile and press Move Out: their vehicles are unassigned and their parking is released.

      Approve resident changes

      Community → Pending Resident Changes lists details residents asked to change. Approve or reject each.

      Staff

      Operations → Staff: add staff one by one or import a list. A login is created for each staff member, so everyone has their own. You can view, reset, disable or enable a staff login from the staff page.

      Users and roles

      Administration → Users & Roles: add users, give each a role, reset a forgotten password, suspend someone who left. The Permission Matrix and Forms Matrix show and change which role can use which screen.

      Maintenance bills

      manager · treasurer · committee

      How each flat's bill is worked out, and the monthly routine to issue it.

      Elements→Charge heads→Billing cycle→Preview→Generate→Issue→Payments

      Maintenance Elements

      Finance → Maintenance Elements is the list of things the society charges for: Service Charges, Security, Housekeeping, Lift, Common Electricity, Water, Property Tax, Sinking Fund, Repairs Fund, Insurance, Parking and more. The standard list is ready; edit it or add your own.

      Charge heads and rules

      Finance → Maintenance Billing has four tabs: Cycles, Charge Heads, Rules and Fines & Charges. A charge head says how an element is shared among flats:

      BasisYou enterEach flat pays
      Same for every flat₹ per flat per monthThat amount
      Per sq ft₹ per sq ft per monthRate × flat area
      % of construction cost% per yearArea × cost per sq ft × % ÷ 12
      Annual budget, split equally₹ per yearBudget ÷ flats ÷ 12
      Annual budget, split by area₹ per yearBudget × flat area ÷ total area ÷ 12
      Per parking slot₹ per slot per monthRate × the flat's slots

      Under Rules set the construction cost per sq ft (needed for the sinking and repair funds), the interest on late payment, grace days, the extra percentage for flats let to tenants (non-occupancy, 0 to 10%), and GST if the society is registered.

      Budget from expenses: switch it on for a charge head and the bill uses what the society actually spent on that element over the last months. Suggest from expenses shows the figures first. Nothing changes until you apply it, and bills already made are never recalculated.

      Make and issue a bill

      1. Maintenance Billing → Cycles → New cycle for the period, for example “October 2026”.
      2. Preview shows every flat's bill with warnings (a flat with no area, no construction cost). Fix those first.
      3. Generate creates the bills, then Issue sends them to the members.

      Fines and extra charges on one flat

      Maintenance Billing → Fines & Charges: add a fine or an extra charge to one flat with a reason and a start date, once or on every bill until an end date. The member is notified.

      Record payments

      Open Finance → Payments → Record Payment, or Record Payment inside a bill. Pick the flat, choose Open bills, oldest first or One bill, enter the payment details and attach a photo of the cheque or receipt, then Save & Generate Receipt. A payment settles the oldest bill first. Extra money becomes an advance for the next bill. If a payment is rejected, it is taken back out of the books.

      Defaulters and bank matching

      • Finance → Defaulters ages the dues from the due date. Three months overdue makes a member a defaulter. Send reminders from here.
      • Finance → Bank Reconciliation: match your bank statement to the recorded payments. A bounced cheque reopens the bills it had paid.

      Daily expenses and accounts

      manager · treasurer · committee

      Where to put daily and monthly expenses, and how they count towards each maintenance element.

      Add an expense

      Use this for anything the society spends: a petty-cash purchase today, or this month's security, electricity or lift bill.

      1. Open Finance → Accounts and press Add Expense. (It is also on Maintenance Elements as Add expense.)
      2. Choose the date and what it is for (the maintenance element: Security, Lift, Housekeeping…). Pick Other if it is not billed through maintenance.
      3. Choose the expense head. If the element has one head, it is chosen for you.
      4. Enter the amount and Paid from (cash in hand or a bank account). Add who was paid, the bill number and a note if you like.
      5. Press Save expense, or Save and add another for a batch of small bills.
      It links itselfSaving adds a payment voucher to the books and links the expense head to the element you chose, so the spend counts towards that element from then on. You do not link anything by hand.

      Monthly bills are entered each month: the app does not repeat them for you. If the expense head you need does not exist, add it first under Accounts → Chart of Accounts.

      See spend by element

      Accounts → Spend by Element shows, for a month or the year so far, what was spent on each element and on which heads. An element with no entry says Nothing recorded, which makes a missed month easy to spot. Tap a head to open its ledger. At the bottom, Not counted towards any element lists spend on heads no element covers, with a Link… button to assign each.

      Your books

      The Accounts page opens with Bank Balance, Cash in Hand, Members' Dues, Payable to Vendors, and this year's Income and Expenditure. Below that:

      Day Book

      Every voucher, date-wise. Print it for any period.

      Vouchers

      Receipt, Payment, Contra and Journal. You can correct one while its year is open, with a reason.

      Chart of Accounts

      The list of ledgers. Edit an expense ledger to choose which element it counts towards.

      Reports

      Trial Balance, Income & Expenditure, Balance Sheet, Receipts & Payments, Schedule of Funds. Each can be a PDF.

      Bills and payments post to the books on their own. If something recorded earlier is missing, press Post now on the Accounts page.

      Closing the year

      After 31 March, admin or committee closes the year in order, oldest first. A closed year is locked, so nothing can be entered in it. The latest closed year can be reopened with a reason.

      Vendors and work orders

      manager · committee

      Operations → Vendors & Work has four tabs: Work Orders, Contracts, Vendors and Limits.

      One-time work, step by step

      1. Create the work order (manager or committee) with a title, vendor and expected cost.
      2. Add quotations from different vendors.
      3. The committee sanctions it. The Limits tab holds the society's spending limits that decide what an order needs.
      4. Issue the work order to the vendor. A printed copy is available.
      5. Record the vendor's bills against it, then record each payment.
      6. The committee certifies completion, then releases any retention and closes the order.

      Annual contracts (AMC)

      The Contracts tab keeps yearly contracts such as the lift or the security agency, with their dates and amounts. Bills against them go to the right expense head.

      Vendors

      The Vendors tab is the vendor master: company, category, contact, and a blacklist for vendors you no longer use. Enter any other vendor bill under Finance → Vendor Bills and pick its expense head. It then counts towards the matching element.

      Parking

      admin · committee

      Operations → Parking Management has three tabs.

      Slots

      Add a zone (Basement, Open…) and then its slots.

      Allocations

      Who holds which slot. Press Release to give it back.

      Vehicles

      Every registered vehicle. It opens on No parking, the vehicles still waiting. Search by plate, owner or flat.

      Give a vehicle a slot

      1. Open the Vehicles tab. It shows the vehicles with no parking.
      2. Press Allot parking beside the vehicle and pick a free slot.
      3. For rented parking, add the monthly charge and an end date. When the end date passes, the parking stops counting at once and the slot is free again.

      One vehicle holds one slot, and one slot goes to one vehicle. The guard's Vehicle Gate screen reads this list.

      Devices and security

      everyone

      You can use your login on several devices, and you stay in control of them.

      Signed-in devices

      Open your account menu → Signed-in devices. It lists every device signed in with your login, with the last time each was used and a mark on This device. Press Sign out beside one, or Sign out other devices to keep only this one. Do this at once if you lose a phone or left a computer signed in.

      Good to know

      • Sign out ends your session on the server too, not only on your screen.
      • Changing your password signs out your other devices. An admin reset signs out all of them.
      • After 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes, sign-in is paused for that login for the rest of the 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
      • Not opening the app for 7 days signs you out. Just sign in again.
      • If you see “You were signed out. Please sign in again.”, another device or the admin ended your session.
      • Keep your own login private. A login used by several people hides who did what.

      Quick answers

      everyone

      The questions people ask most.

      Where do I enter daily expenses?

      Finance → Accounts → Add Expense. Pick what it is for, the head, the amount, and cash or bank. It counts towards that maintenance element on its own.

      Where do I enter the monthly security, electricity or lift bill?

      In the same place: Add Expense, once a month, choosing the element. If the bill comes from a vendor you have a work order or contract with, enter it as a Vendor Bill instead.

      How do I see what we spent on each element?

      Accounts → Spend by Element, for a month or the year so far.

      A bill did not change after we spent more. Why?

      The charge head is not on Budget from expenses, the expense head is not linked to its element, or the bill was already generated. Bills already generated are never recalculated.

      I cannot see a screen my colleague sees.

      Screens depend on role. Ask the admin to check Administration → Forms Matrix.

      The guard says my vehicle is “Registered, no parking”.

      Your vehicle is on the list but has no slot. Ask the committee to give it one under Parking Management → Vehicles.

      Do empty flats pay less maintenance?

      No. Expenses are shared among all members. Only flats let to a tenant can pay more, through the non-occupancy percentage.

      I forgot my password.

      Ask the admin to reset it under Users & Roles. If you are the admin and are locked out, the platform team can reset it for you.

      Not in the app yet

      • Amenity booking, notices and inventory have no screens yet.
      • Reading a number plate from a camera photo. The gate check uses a typed or scanned number.
      • Monthly expenses that repeat by themselves. Enter them each month.
      • A parking charge that goes into the maintenance bill on its own. The monthly charge is recorded only.
      • A one-time password (OTP) at sign-in.
      Nothing matches that. Try a shorter word, such as “bill” or “visitor”.
